# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Felu Modak Next.js Brand Experience

## 1. Product Vision & Value Proposition

**Felu Modak** is a legendary confectionery based in Rishra, Hooghly, West Bengal, with a rich history of over 175 years. Reaching patrons like Mahatma Gandhi, Rabindranath Tagore, and Subhas Chandra Bose, the shop is a landmark of Bengali sweet-making craft.

### The Objective
Transform the legacy website of Felu Modak into a premium, responsive, single-page D2C brand experience built on **Next.js**. 
- Replace complex, resource-heavy WebGL/Three.js integrations with highly polished, high-resolution food photography assets and clean grid structures.
- Focus on authentic, web-crawled history and signature product details.
- Provide a smooth, accessible D2C order tray, allowing customers to schedule cold-chain shipments and customize packaging engravings.

---

## 2. Technical Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router, static site generation optimized).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 (inline theme configurations, CSS variables).
- **Icons:** Lucide React.
- **Interactions:** Framer Motion and clean CSS transitions.
- **Order Integrations:** Client-side state cart drawer, scheduled deliveries, custom packaging, canvas-confetti success triggers.

---

## 3. Product Catalog (Crawled Content)
1. **Mohini Sandesh (Jolbhora):** Felu Modak's flagship sweet. Soft cottage cheese shell holding a liquid jaggery (Nolen Gur) or rose syrup center.
2. **Saffron Rabri:** Saffron-infused, slow-simmered whole milk layered with cream folds and garnished with almonds and pistachios.
3. **Heritage Gaja:** Multi-layered dough fried in pure ghee and soaked in cardamom sugar syrup.
4. **Chhanar Polao:** Tiny fried paneer globules resembling rice grains, tossed with raisins and saffron.
